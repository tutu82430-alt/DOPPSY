#### 1. Purpose of the Pilot Partnership
The purpose of this 60-Day Pilot is to validate Doppsy’s zero-wait-time concierge scheduling technology, automate WhatsApp patient confirmations, reduce clinic no-shows, and provide verified patients with guaranteed consultation windows.

#### 2. Term & Scope
* **Duration:** Sixty (60) calendar days commencing from the Go-Live Date.
* **Scope of Practice:** Listing of Provider’s daily consultation roster on Doppsy Web and Mobile App, with automated 2-way WhatsApp appointment concierge notifications.

#### 3. Financial Terms (Zero Cost Guarantee)
* **Onboarding & Subscription Fee:** **₹0 (100% Free)** during the 60-day pilot.
* **Zero Platform Commission:** Doppsy will NOT charge any commission on consultation fees during this pilot period.
* **Consultation Fees:** The patient’s regular consultation fee (e.g. ₹500 - ₹1,500) will be collected directly by the clinic reception desk as per existing clinic standard practice.
* **No Lock-in:** The Provider may pause or conclude participation at any time with forty-eight (48) hours prior written or digital notice.

#### 4. Obligations of Doppsy
1. **Automated WhatsApp Concierge:** Dispatches immediate booking confirmation, 24-hour reminder, and real-time cancellation alerts to patients without requiring reception staff manual calls.
2. **Dedicated Mobile Roster:** Provides the doctor and reception team with real-time access to the mobile dashboard (`/doctor`) to view the daily patient queue, check-in status, and mark attendance.
3. **Queue Discipline:** Limits bookings strictly to designated zero-wait consultation intervals to prevent clinic waiting room congestion.
4. **Physical Reception Collateral:** Supplies complimentary high-quality counter acrylic standees with direct clinic-specific QR codes for follow-up patients.

#### 5. Obligations of the Provider & Clinic
1. **Punctuality & Zero-Wait Adherence:** Respect the designated appointment slot window for patients arriving via Doppsy.
2. **Roster Status Updates:** Receptionist or Doctor taps "Mark Completed" or "Mark No-Show" on the mobile dashboard upon consultation conclusion.
3. **Emergency Unavailability Notice:** In the event of emergency surgeries or unplanned leave, notify Doppsy or block out unavailable slots via the dashboard at least 2 hours in advance.

#### 6. Patient Data Protection & Compliance
1. Both parties agree to handle patient health information in compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023)** and relevant telemedicine practice guidelines.
2. Doppsy shall not sell, harvest, or utilize patient medical records for unsolicited third-party commercial marketing.

#### 2. Reception Desk SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)

| Scenario | Reception Action | Time Required |
|---|---|---|
| **Patient Arrives at Clinic** | Check patient name on Doppsy Mobile Roster (`/doctor`). Collect regular clinic consultation fee. Mark patient as arrived. | 15 seconds |
| **Patient Finishes Consultation** | Tap **"Mark Completed"** (green button) on mobile dashboard. | 3 seconds |

| **Patient Needs Follow-up in 7 Days** | Point patient to the reception counter QR Standee: *"Scan this on WhatsApp to lock in your follow-up slot without calling."* | 10 seconds |
| **Doctor Delayed by Emergency** | Access portal -> Tap "Block Slots" or notify Doppsy Concierge helpline on WhatsApp. | 30 seconds |
